\section*{Dataset construction}
\subsection*{Phase 1 --- Gaussian (closed-form ground truth)}
Scalar base: $X_1,X_2\sim\mathcal N(0,1)$ with $\mathrm{corr}(X_1,X_2)=\rho$; label $Y=X_1+X_2+\sigma_y\varepsilon$, $\varepsilon\sim\mathcal N(0,1)$. Thus $\operatorname{Var}(Y)=2+2\rho+\sigma_y^2$ and $\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,Y)=1+\rho$. To lift to $d$ dimensions the informative scalar is padded with $d-1$ i.i.d.\ $\mathcal N(0,1)$ coordinates and rotated by a fixed per-seed orthogonal matrix $Q_i$ (one per variable). Rotation and independent padding preserve mutual information, so $S$ is unchanged and the closed form carries over. Ground truth is computed from the $3\times3$ covariance via $I=\tfrac12\log\!\big(\det\Sigma_A\det\Sigma_B/\det\Sigma_{AB}\big)$ and the analogous conditional form. Cells sweep $\rho\in\{-.9,\dots,.9\}$, $\sigma_y\in\{.1,1\}$, $n\in\{1\text{k},\dots,100\text{k}\}$, $d\in\{1,8,32,128\}$.
\subsection*{Phase 2 --- discrete U/R/S (mixture-density MC truth)}
Latent sources $s_1,s_2,s_3\sim\mathrm{Unif}\{0,\dots,Q-1\}$ with $Q=8$. Encodings $X_i=W_i\,\mathrm{onehot}(s_i)+\sigma_{\mathrm{enc}}\,\mathcal N(0,I_{32})$ with orthonormal $W_i\in\mathbb R^{32\times8}$ frozen per seed. Regimes (X$_1$ always encodes $s_1$): \textbf{UNIQUE} $Y{=}s_1$; \textbf{REDUNDANT} $Y{=}s_1$ and X$_2$ also encodes $s_1$; \textbf{SYNERGY1} $Y{=}(s_1{+}s_2)\bmod Q$ with X$_2\!\sim\!s_2$; \textbf{SYNERGY2} $Y{=}(s_3{+}(s_1{+}s_2)\bmod Q)\bmod Q$ with X$_2{=}[\mathrm{enc}(s_2)\,|\,\mathrm{enc}(s_3)]\in\mathbb R^{64}$; \textbf{INDEPENDENT} $Y{=}s_3$. The $\lambda$-dial mixes the SYNERGY1 and REDUNDANT rules per sample (Bernoulli($\lambda$)). Ground truth is the exact $S=I(X_1;X_2,Y)-I(X_1;X_2)-I(X_1;Y)$ obtained by Monte Carlo over the enumerable Gaussian-mixture components (matches the discrete limit as $\sigma_{\mathrm{enc}}\!\to\!0$: $\pm\log Q$ for pure synergy/redundancy, $0$ for unique/independent).
\section*{Method: SynDiff}
Target $S=I(X_1;Y\mid X_2)-I(X_1;Y)$ in nats. By the symmetry of interaction information this equals $I(X_1;X_2\mid Y)-I(X_1;X_2)$, which the four-mask form below estimates directly. \textbf{VP schedule:} $\beta(t)=0.1+19.9t$, $B(t)=\int_0^t\!\beta=0.1t+9.95t^2$, signal $k(t)=e^{-B/2}$, noise $\sigma^2(t)=1-e^{-B}$, weight $w(t)=\beta(t)/(2\sigma^2(t))$. A single noise-prediction network $\hat\varepsilon(x_{1,t},t,\mathrm{cond})$ (\emph{MaskedCondMLP}: Fourier-$t$ features $\oplus$ per-slot conditioning embeddings each with a learned NULL token; $4\times512$ SiLU) is trained to denoise $X_1$ while each conditioning slot (X$_2$, $Y$) is independently dropped to its NULL token with probability $0.15$. EMA weights (decay $0.999$) are used at evaluation.
\textbf{Four-mask estimator.} Per evaluation triple and per stratified time $t$ ($K{=}16$ strata on $[10^{-3},1]$) with \emph{one shared} noise draw $\varepsilon$ and $x_{1,t}=k(t)x_1+\sigma(t)\varepsilon$:
\begin{align*}e_1&=\hat\varepsilon(x_{1,t},t,x_2,y), & e_2&=\hat\varepsilon(x_{1,t},t,\varnothing,y),\\e_3&=\hat\varepsilon(x_{1,t},t,x_2,\varnothing), & e_4&=\hat\varepsilon(x_{1,t},t,\varnothing,\varnothing),\\\text{integrand}(t)&=w(t)\big(\|e_1-e_2\|^2-\|e_3-e_4\|^2\big).&&\end{align*}
Per-sample $\hat S=(1-10^{-3})\,\overline{\text{integrand}}_t$; the estimate is the mean over $10^4$ fresh triples with a 1000-sample bootstrap CI (norms in fp32, no-grad, EMA weights). \textbf{Baselines:} \emph{minde\_comp} (three separately trained MINDE MI terms composed as above), \emph{MINE} and \emph{InfoNCE} (lower-bound MI, same composition), \emph{batch\_pid} (Liang et al.\ CVX PID, k-means $k{=}20$ then max-entropy solve), and \emph{broja}/\emph{plugin} on $d{=}1$.
